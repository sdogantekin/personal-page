Product Builder · Berlin

Hi, I'm Serkan.

Product builder. With ❤️ from Berlin, Germany.

I usually describe myself as a product person with engineering roots. I started my professional journey as a software engineer, grew into an engineering leadership role, and eventually moved into product management — where I get to bring both worlds together: technical judgment and product instinct.

Outside of my day job, I like building small things for fun. One of them is CV Butler, a tool that runs an ATS review on your résumé, scores it against a job description, and writes you a cover letter. Another is Taxi Station, a web-based taxi station management game set in Istanbul. I also write about product and engineering on Medium.

Want the fuller story? Check out my resume, or just say hi.
